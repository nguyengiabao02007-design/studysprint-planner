🧭What is StudySprint?

StudySprint is a lightweight study-planning companion for students balancing classes, assignments, and revision. It helps make the next step clear when a to-do list feels too big.

You stay in charge of your learning. The planner organizes your tasks; it does not do your schoolwork for you.

✅What it can and cannot do

✓ StudySprint can

  • Help organize a personal study list
  • Suggest a simple order and time split
  • Show your progress as you work

× StudySprint cannot

  • Replace a teacher, tutor, or your own judgment
  • Guarantee a grade or complete assignments for you
  • Know your deadlines unless you add them
